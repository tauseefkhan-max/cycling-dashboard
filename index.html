About these numbers
FTP (264 W) is a 2-parameter critical-power estimate from the best 1- and 3-minute power of the last six weeks, so it moves with current form. NP is normalized power from each ride's raw power stream; FTP and VO₂ in the rides table are estimated from that ride's own best efforts, so easy rides read low. Files with power-meter glitches are excluded from records. The calorie columns convert kcal into gulab jamun (~150 kcal) and jalebi (~380 kcal per 100 g), just for fun. Updated hourly from Strava.
